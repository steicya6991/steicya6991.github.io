Context

After finishing our learning in how to code of basic HTML and CSS, my Sep teacher Mr.Muller wanted us to practice our skills by doing a creavtive project. In this project, we were required to create a movie web project about any fictional movie by using our understanding of the basic HtML and CSS. So, with my partner and I choose to do comedy and we named it "SEP Revenge".

Content

After choosing what we were going to do, we were instruced to create a plan. So, we listed in what we had to do then we divided the work. Then, we are also giving the opition to create a sequence and we did. The first movie is about how Sep class took revenge on Mr. Muller before spring break and the second movie is about Mr.Muller getting back his revenge. There was a list of elements that we needed to include in our project.

The second day of the project was more working, I kept asking him about in what he thinks about this or that. Also, like to add the efficiently between me and my pratner were he would like stop doing a bit of the work.
